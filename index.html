Chapter 03

The Brewing Guide

Small details make a difference. Start with these basic guidelines.

☕

Espresso

Starting point: About 18 g of ground coffee for a double espresso. A typical starting yield is around 36 g in 25–30 seconds. Adjust to your beans and machine.

♨

Pour-Over

Starting point: 15 g coffee to 250 g water. Try water around 92–96°C and a total brew time of about 2.5–4 minutes.

🍃

Loose-Leaf Tea

Starting point: About 2 g tea per 200 ml water. Adjust the temperature and steeping time to the specific tea variety.

Black Tea

95–100°C · Usually 3–5 minutes. Use boiling water and adjust steeping time to taste.

Green Tea

Usually 70–85°C · About 1–3 minutes. Cooler water can help prevent excessive bitterness.

White Tea

Usually 75–85°C · About 2–5 minutes. Delicate teas may benefit from gentler water temperatures.

Oolong Tea

About 85–95°C · Start with 2–4 minutes. Rolled and roasted varieties may need different methods.

Herbal Infusions

Usually 95–100°C · About 5–10 minutes. Follow the instructions for the specific herbs and ingredients.

Chapter 04

Easy Drink Recipes

Simple favorites to prepare at home.

🥛

Vanilla Latte

Ingredients: 1 espresso, 180–220 ml milk, vanilla syrup to taste.

Method: Brew espresso, steam the milk, add vanilla, and pour the milk into the coffee.

🧊

Iced Americano

Ingredients: 1–2 espresso shots, cold water, ice.

Method: Fill a glass with ice, add water, and pour the espresso over it. Adjust strength to taste.

🍵

Matcha Latte

Ingredients: 1–2 g matcha, 60 ml warm water, 180 ml milk.

Method: Sift and whisk matcha with water until smooth. Add steamed or cold milk.

🍋

Lemon Iced Tea

Ingredients: 250 ml brewed black tea, lemon, ice, optional sweetener.

Method: Brew and cool the tea. Pour over ice, add lemon, and sweeten to taste.

🍫

Mocha

Ingredients: 1 espresso, 1–2 teaspoons cocoa or chocolate sauce, 180 ml milk.

Method: Mix chocolate into the hot espresso, then add steamed milk.

🌿

Mint Herbal Tea

Ingredients: Fresh mint leaves and 250 ml hot water.

Method: Pour hot water over mint and steep for 5–7 minutes. Add lemon if desired.

Chapter 05

Little Tips, Better Flavor

01. Fresh Ingredients

Store coffee in an airtight container away from heat, moisture, and sunlight. Buy tea from a reliable source and keep it dry.

02. Water Matters

Use fresh, good-tasting water. Water quality and temperature can change the flavor of both coffee and tea.

03. Experiment

Change one variable at a time: grind size, water temperature, steeping time, or the amount of coffee or tea.
